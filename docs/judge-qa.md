# Judge Q&A

## Demo close

“ClearOwn. Document in, AI extraction with MiniMax, live registry verification, risk detection, and a portable on-chain credential. We turn a 5-day manual process into a 30-second workflow.”

## Why blockchain?

“KYB verification is redundant across institutions. Every bank, fintech, and VC repeats the same investigation independently. Our on-chain credential means a company gets verified once and can present proof to every future counterparty. It’s the same logic as a SOC 2 report, but cryptographically verifiable.”

## Is this the real ownership chain?

“These are related entities detected through registry data and structured extraction. In production, each node gets resolved through deeper officer lookups and cross-jurisdiction registry queries. The demo shows the automated detection and risk-flagging layer, which is the part that is still manual today.”

## What is your moat?

“Registry integrations. Every jurisdiction has a different API, different format, and different access rules. Today we show the workflow on top of live registry-style verification. The more registries we connect, the deeper our traversal and the stronger our product gets.”

## Who pays for this?

“Banks, fintechs, exchanges, and VC firms. Any institution onboarding business counterparties runs KYB. A single complex investigation can cost $1,500 to $5,000 today. We charge a fraction of that and return results much faster.”

## What did you actually build?

“Lovable for the product experience, n8n for the recursive workflow, MiniMax for AI extraction, Replit for the hackathon mint backend, and Crossmint on Base Sepolia for the portable credential.”
