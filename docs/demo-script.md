# Demo Script

## 4-minute version

### 0:00 - 0:30

“Every bank, VC, exchange, and fintech onboarding a business has to answer one question: who actually controls this entity? Today that process is manual, repetitive, and easy to hide behind shell-company layers. ClearOwn automates that investigation.”

### 0:30 - 1:00

Show landing page.

“We start with a business entity and jurisdiction. In production this can come from uploaded formation or ownership documents.”

### 1:00 - 1:45

Click `Launch Investigation`.

“ClearOwn triggers an agentic workflow that extracts ownership data, recursively traverses the next layer, and flags risky jurisdictions as it goes.”

### 1:45 - 2:45

Show results page.

Call out:

- UBO found
- chain depth
- high-risk jurisdictions
- ownership chain visualization

### 2:45 - 3:20

Click `Issue KYB Passport`.

“Once the UBO is verified, we issue a portable KYB credential rather than forcing the next institution to redo the audit from scratch.”

### 3:20 - 4:00

Show credential card and BaseScan.

“This is now cryptographically anchored on Base Sepolia through Crossmint. The investigation result becomes portable, timestamped, and externally verifiable.”
